# Design Direction

## Identity
- **Product**: DepEd Division HRIS Portal (Human Resource Information System)
- **Purpose**: Division-level management of employee records, CSC Form 6 leave applications, Form 48 daily time records (DTR), and service records.
- **Dial**: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 (Institutional, utilitarian public-service application)

## Personality
- **Voice**: Institutional, utilitarian, authoritative, calm
- **Standard**: WCAG AA compliance, content-first clarity, keyboard-navigable administrative workflow

## Palette
- **Dominant (Primary)**: DepEd Blue (#0038A8)
- **Supporting Neutral**: Slate Neutral (#0F172A text, #475569 muted text, #E2E8F0 border, #F8FAFC page background)
- **Deliberate Accent**: DepEd Gold (#FCD116) - used sparingly for key action highlights and pending status badges
- **Semantic States**: Success Green (#15803D), Error Red (#B91C1C), Warning Amber (#B45309)

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter / System UI sans-serif (`font-sans`)
- **Figures / Code**: Tabular numbers for dates, salaries, and attendance records
- **Hierarchy**: Functional contrast with explicit font weight and size, avoiding arbitrary decorative typography

## Mood & Structure
- **Density**: Dense administrative dashboard with structured data tables, clear Form 6 / Form 48 layouts, and high-contrast inputs
- **Motion**: Functional state transitions and hover/focus cues only (no decorative floating, bounce, or gratuitous scroll effects)
- **Integrity**: Real DepEd / CSC forms and terminology only; no fake statistics or decorative placeholder cards
